# Privacy Policy for PhotoBook

**Last Updated: October 28, 2024**

## Introduction

PhotoBook ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS application.

## Information We Collect

### Photos and Media
- **What we collect**: The app accesses photos from your device's photo library that you explicitly select for creating photobooks.
- **Why we collect it**: To create your custom photobook layouts and generate print-ready files.
- **How we use it**: Photos are processed locally on your device for quality analysis and layout generation.

### Photo Metadata
- **What we collect**: Creation dates, location data (if available), and technical photo information (resolution, orientation).
- **Why we collect it**: To organize photos chronologically and optimize layout generation.
- **How we use it**: All metadata processing happens locally on your device.

## How We Use Your Information

### Local Processing
- All photo analysis and quality scoring happens entirely on your device
- No photos are uploaded to our servers
- Layout generation and preview rendering occur locally

### Print Service Integration
- Photos are only transmitted to third-party print services when you explicitly place an order
- Each print service has its own privacy policy (see links below)
- We do not store or retain copies of your photos on our servers

## Data Sharing

### Third-Party Print Services
When you submit an order, your photos and photobook design are shared with your chosen print service:
- **Shutterfly**: [Privacy Policy](https://www.shutterfly.com/about/privacy-policy)
- **Mixbook**: [Privacy Policy](https://www.mixbook.com/privacy)
- **Chatbooks**: [Privacy Policy](https://chatbooks.com/privacy)
- **Custom Print Services**: Privacy policies vary by provider

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party print services. Please review their privacy policies before placing orders.

### No Sale of Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or photos to any third parties.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Selected photos remain in your device's photo library
- Photobook layouts and preferences may be stored locally on your device
- No cloud synchronization (unless you enable iCloud Photos through iOS Settings)

### Security Measures
- All data transmission to print services uses encrypted HTTPS connections
- We implement industry-standard security practices
- Your photos are never stored on our servers

## Your Rights and Choices

### Photo Access Control
- You can revoke photo library access at any time through iOS Settings
- The app will stop functioning without photo access, as it's essential to the service

### Data Deletion
- You can delete the app at any time to remove all locally stored data
- Deleting the app does not affect photos in your photo library
- Contact print services directly to request deletion of submitted orders

### Location Data
- If your photos contain location metadata, it may be used for chronological organization
- Location data is never transmitted separately or used for tracking
- You can disable location services for Camera in iOS Settings

## Children's Privacy

PhotoBook does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Analytics and Tracking

**Current Status**: PhotoBook does not currently use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

If we add analytics in the future:
- We will update this policy
- You will be notified within the app
- You will have the option to opt-out

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Significant changes will be communicated through:
- In-app notification
- App Store update notes

## California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under CCPA:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

## International Users

PhotoBook is designed for users in the United States. If you use the app from other countries, your information may be transferred to the United States or the country where your chosen print service operates.
